ACCOUNTING MECHANICS // MODULE 01.8

Working Capital Physics: Why Profitable Businesses Still Run Out of Cash

BY EDITORIAL DESK (FICTIONAL PERSONA) • PUBLISHED 23 OCT 2026 • 12 MIN READ

Accrual accounting records revenue when a product is delivered, not when the customer's cash arrives in the bank. Because of this timing gap, a rapidly growing company can report record accounting profits on its Income Statement while simultaneously experiencing a severe liquidity crunch .

[Margin Note 01 — Accrual vs. Cash]: Under IFRS and UK GAAP, revenue is recognised when performance obligations are satisfied, even if the invoice allows 60 or 90 days for cash settlement.

To diagnose this dynamic, analysts measure the Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): the number of days a pound of capital remains trapped in physical inventory and unpaid customer invoices before returning as cash .

[Margin Note 02 — CCC Formula]: CCC = Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) + Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) − Days Payable Outstanding (DPO).
"Growth is not free. When a business requires 95 days of working capital to fulfil orders, doubling sales requires doubling the cash locked inside warehouses."

Analytical Ledger: Evidence, Interpretation & Unknowns

Rather than issuing simplistic "Buy" or "Sell" calls, rigorous financial research separates observable balance-sheet facts from analytical inferences and irreducible uncertainties.

01. Observable Evidence [FACTS]
  • Gross Margin Stability: Hypothetical filings show gross margins stayed between 56.0% and 57.5% across five synthetic fiscal years.
  • Zero Net Debt: Cash and short-term gilts (£42m) exceed total lease and bank obligations (£14m).
  • R&D Expensing: 100% of software engineering costs are expensed on the income statement rather than capitalised.
02. Analytical Interpretation [MECHANICS]
  • Pricing Power Signal: Stable gross margins during input-cost inflation indicate customer switching costs in regulated factory lines.
  • Unlevered ROE Quality: Because the Equity Multiplier is only 1.30x, the 20.6% ROE is driven by operating margins, not debt amplification.
  • Conservative Accounting: Expensing all R&D depresses near-term stated operating profit but prevents intangible asset bloat.
03. Key Unknowns & Risks [UNCERTAINTY]
  • Reinvestment Runway: Can the company deploy retained cash at 20%+ incremental returns, or is its niche market saturated?
  • Technological Obsolescence: Optical sensor architectures could displace legacy mechanical pressure gauges over a 10-year cycle.
  • Customer Concentration: Top three industrial OEM buyers represent 34% of hypothetical annual billings.
